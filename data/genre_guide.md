# Genre and Mood Guide

Each `##` heading is a genre or mood name that appears in songs.csv.

# Genres

## ambient
Slow, spacious, texture-driven music with little rhythm or melody. People put it on for sleep, meditation, or quiet background focus.

## blues
Guitar-led music built on bent notes, often a 12-bar pattern, and emotional, story-like vocals. People listen when they want something soulful, reflective, or bittersweet.

## classical
Orchestral or solo-instrument music, usually acoustic and instrumental, with wide dynamic range. People listen for concentration, relaxation, or an elegant evening.

## country
Acoustic guitar, steady rhythms, and plain-spoken storytelling about home, love, and small-town life. It suits road trips and easygoing evenings.

## edm
Electronic dance music with a pounding beat, big synth builds, and drops. People play it at parties, festivals, and during high-energy workouts.

## folk
Mostly acoustic guitar and voice with simple, heartfelt songwriting. It fits quiet evenings, nostalgia, and unplugged moods.

## hip-hop
Rhythm-first music built on beats and rapped vocals, often confident and lyric-heavy. People listen while commuting, working out, or hanging out with friends.

## indie pop
Light, melodic pop with a homemade, guitar-and-synth feel. It suits sunny afternoons, walks, and casual feel-good listening.

## jazz
Improvised, swinging music with rich chords and instruments like piano, sax, and upright bass. People enjoy it over dinner, while reading, or late at night.

## latin
Warm, rhythmic music with percussion and dance-friendly grooves. It brings energy to parties, dancing, and summer days.

## lofi
Mellow, lo-fi beats with soft keys, vinyl crackle, and a slow steady tempo, mostly instrumental. People play it for studying, coding, and relaxing in the background.

## metal
Loud, fast music with distorted guitars, heavy drums, and powerful vocals. People listen for intense workouts, release, and high adrenaline.

## pop
Catchy, polished songs with big hooks and clear vocals. It works almost anywhere: parties, car rides, and everyday listening.

## r&b
Smooth, groove-based music with soulful vocals and a focus on romance and feeling. It suits late evenings, dates, and relaxed hangouts.

## reggae
Laid-back music with an offbeat guitar rhythm and a deep bassline. People listen to unwind, relax outdoors, or set a sunny mood.

## rock
Guitar-driven music with a strong backbeat and a lot of attitude. It fits driving, workouts, and anytime you want energy and edge.

## synthwave
Retro 1980s-style electronic music with analog synths and a steady, driving pulse. It suits night drives, coding sessions, and moody, neon-lit atmospheres.

# Moods

## aggressive
Fierce, forceful music with heavy drums and a sharp edge. People reach for it to release anger or push through a hard workout.

## chill
Calm, easygoing music with a slow tempo and soft sounds. It suits studying, unwinding, and low-key background listening.

## confident
Strong, swaggering music with a steady beat and a bold attitude. People use it to feel empowered before a presentation, date, or night out.

## energetic
Fast, upbeat music that raises your pulse and motivation. It suits workouts, running, and getting ready to go out.

## euphoric
Soaring, joyful music with big builds and a feeling of release. It fits dance floors, festivals, and celebrations.

## focused
Steady, unobtrusive music with little lyrical distraction. People play it for studying, reading, and deep work.

## happy
Bright, upbeat music with major-key melodies and a positive feel. It suits sunny days, errands, and lifting your mood.

## intense
Driving, high-pressure music with powerful rhythm and drama. It fits hard workouts, action, and moments that need momentum.

## melancholic
Slow, sorrowful music with minor-key tones, for sadness and reflection. People listen on rainy days, during quiet loneliness, or when they want to feel understood.

## moody
Dark, atmospheric music with shadowy textures and a brooding feel. It suits late-night drives, rainy evenings, and introspective hours.

## nostalgic
Warm, familiar-sounding music that recalls earlier times. People listen when reminiscing, looking at old photos, or winding down.

## relaxed
Gentle, unhurried music with a loose groove. It fits weekends, lounging, and easing out of a busy day.

## romantic
Tender, intimate music about love and closeness. It suits dates, dinners, and slow evenings with someone special.

## serene
Peaceful, quiet music that feels still and open. People use it for meditation, sleep, and calm mornings.

## wistful
Gently sad, longing music with a hint of warmth. It suits looking back, quiet walks, and reflective evenings.
